From the editor

This one’s close to my heart.

This is a project I’ve wanted to build for so long. I loved Paperboy on the Game Boy, and doing a paper round was one of my first ever jobs, so this one’s close to my heart. For the longest time I’ve wanted to remake something similar. This game is completely inspired by it, but it isn’t a copy. I wanted my own take on the concept, including the art direction.

Back when Fable first released in July, I had a stab at it, thinking it might be the first time I could actually pull it off. I burned a lot of time and didn’t get very far. Then Astra came out, and that changed everything. When I jumped into Astra I gave it the original game as a reference, and it almost one-shotted it in its own art style. But that was never really the vision I had for this Paperboy game. I wanted something more 3D and immersive, with a much richer visual experience, so I changed the angle and made it dynamic. It wasn’t without its difficulties. As you can see from this devlog, a hell of a lot of work went into it in a very short space of time: the modelling, the visuals, the rendering, right through to the website itself.

I’ve tried to turn this around as fast as I can, seeing as it caught some wind on X. And unlike most of the demo prototypes I’ve seen flying around on X, I wanted to make something that was actually playable end to end, to show that these new models can, with a bit of effort, steering and focus, create full end-to-end games.

Enjoy.
